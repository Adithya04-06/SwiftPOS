# Design System

## Brand
**SwiftPOS**

## Style
Modern retail POS / SaaS dashboard. Clean, professional, and functional.

## Typography
**Font Family:** Inter

- **Heading 1:** 24px, Bold
- **Heading 2:** 20px, Semi-Bold
- **Heading 3:** 18px, Medium
- **Body:** 14px, Regular
- **Small:** 12px, Regular

## Color Palette

### Primary
- **Primary Blue:** `#2563EB` (Used for primary buttons, active states, links)

### Backgrounds & Surfaces
- **Dark Sidebar:** `#0F172A`
- **Page Background:** `#F8FAFC`
- **Card / Surface:** `#FFFFFF`

### Text
- **Primary Text:** `#0F172A`
- **Secondary Text:** `#64748B`

### Utilities & Borders
- **Border:** `#E2E8F0`

### Semantic Colors
- **Success:** `#16A34A` (In stock, payment success)
- **Warning:** `#F59E0B` (Low stock)
- **Danger:** `#DC2626` (Out of stock, delete actions)

## Spacing System
Base: 8px
- xs: 4px
- sm: 8px
- md: 16px
- lg: 24px
- xl: 32px
- xxl: 48px

## Corner Radius
- **Cards, Modals, Inputs, Buttons:** 12px

## Visual Effects
- **Shadows:** Subtle drop shadow for cards `box-shadow: 0 4px 6px -1px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.1), 0 2px 4px -1px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.06);`
- **Borders:** 1px solid `#E2E8F0` on cards and inputs.
